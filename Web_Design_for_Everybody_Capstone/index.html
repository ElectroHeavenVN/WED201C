About Me

I am a university student from the FPT University who enjoys reverse engineering, electrical engineering, embedded programming, and hands-on DIY projects. I like understanding systems at a low level, from firmware internals to board traces. In fact, my first passion is DIY electronics, before I became interested in programming, and then reverse engineering both hardware and software.

I am particularly interested in the intersection of hardware and software, and I enjoy working on projects that involve both. I believe that a strong understanding of both domains is essential for creating innovative solutions.

Skills

  • Reverse Engineering - my primary focus
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Programming - C#, C++, C, Vue, JavaScript, HTML, CSS and more
  • Software Development - develop software for various platforms
  • Embedded Systems - Arduino, STM32 and more
